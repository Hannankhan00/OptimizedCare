# Design System & Aesthetic Guidelines: Optimized Care

Whenever designing, building, or modifying any UI, components, pages, or styles in this repository, strictly adhere to the **Optimized Care** design specification defined in [DESIGN.md](file:///e:/my%20work/OptimizedCare/DESIGN.md).

## Core Directives for Agent Execution
1. **Always Consult `DESIGN.md`:** Before generating or editing UI code, layouts, components, typography, color tokens, or spacing, reference the design rules in `DESIGN.md`.
2. **Brand & Style:** Minimalist Glass-Tactile & Precision Biotech Optimization. Elevated intersection of modern British healthcare, clinical excellence, and Apple-grade consumer hardware aesthetics.
3. **Palette Enforcement:**
   - Primary: Vibrant Bio-Green (`#29A34A` / `#006b29`)
   - Secondary: Precision Clinical Blue (`#0878B9` / `#00639a`)
   - Tertiary: Deep Imperial Navy (`#071A26` / `#4c5e6c`)
   - Neutral Primary: Deep Slate Carbon (`#102027` / `#0d1e25`)
   - Canvas Base: Serene Off-White / Sky Mist (`#F7F9F8` / `#f4faff`)
   - Clinical Pure White: `#FFFFFF`
   - Delicate Borders: `#E2E8F0` / `#E9EEF0`
   - Outline / Muted: `#6e7a6c` / `#bdcaba`
   - Error: `#ba1a1a` / `#ffdad6`
4. **Typography & Numbers:**
   - Headlines, display tiers, status badges, and telemetry: `Space Grotesk` (`500`, `600`, `700`)
   - Body copy, clinical descriptions, consultations: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`400`, `500`)
   - Tabular numerals (`tnum`) for vitals, prices, time, and clinical measurements.
   - Eyebrows: `label-caps` in `Space Grotesk` with `letterSpacing: 0.08em` and uppercase.
5. **Shapes & Radii:**
   - Buttons, status chips, tags: Full Pill (`9999px` / `rounded-full`)
   - Standard cards & inputs: `32px` (`2rem` / `rounded-lg`)
   - Hero containers & nested modules: `48px` (`3rem` / `rounded-xl`)
   - Concentric nested curvature: outer 32px card houses inner actions with 16px to 20px corners.
6. **Elevation & Glass:** Tonal glass surfaces (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), delicate 1px borders, subtle bio-ambient green/blue halos, avoiding harsh drop shadows.
