## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier visual standard at the intersection of modern British healthcare, clinical excellence, and Apple-grade consumer hardware aesthetics. The visual identity pushes wellness into high-tech biotech optimization: combining electric bio-green highlights, structural clinical navy, pristine geometric titling, and fluid organic curvatures.

### Brand Personality & Tone
- **Precision Optimization:** Unflinching clinical clarity paired with calculated technological warmth.
- **Understated British Luxury:** Quiet confidence expressed through expansive breathing room, micro-fine outlines, and refined surface layers rather than ornamental noise.
- **Biometric Vitality:** Evoking vitality, cell renewal, and peace of mind through organic curvatures that mimic cellular forms, paired with crisp mathematical alignments.

### Visual Style
The design system operates within an elevated **Minimalist Glass-Tactile** framework. It leverages ultra-soft backdrops (`#F7F9F8`), surgical white cards (`#FFFFFF`), whisper-thin slate borders (`#E2E8F0`), and hyper-subtle ambient green and cyan back-glows. High-density data points are delivered with serene legibility, evoking the feel of an advanced diagnostic console redesigned for a high-end wellness retreat.

## Colors

The color palette balances energetic vitality with clinical precision and deep British structural trust.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#29A34A` — Vibrant Bio-Green):** The catalyst for action, health milestones, biological vitality, and active states. Used strategically to draw the eye to critical points of progression and confirmation.
- **Secondary (`#0878B9` — Precision Clinical Blue):** Anchors institutional authority, diagnostics, digital interaction indicators, and tele-health telemetry.
- **Tertiary (`#071A26` — Deep Imperial Navy):** Provides high-contrast anchor blocks, primary buttons, footers, and inverted luxury focus panels.
- **Neutral Primary (`#102027` — Deep Slate Carbon):** The authoritative body text color, offering high contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F7F9F8` — Serene Off-White):** An optical soothing canvas that reduces ocular fatigue and elevates elevated pure-white cards.
- **Border & Structure (`#E2E8F0` / `#E9EEF0` — Subtle Slate/Ice):** Renders micro-delicate 1px borders that separate complex diagnostic modules cleanly.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes technical biotech authority with comfortable clinical reading:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Injects engineered precision and technological forwardness. The subtle geometric quirks and mechanical rhythm give the brand a distinct, high-tech optimization aura without compromising legibility.
- **Body Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Softens the clinical space with warm, highly legible, human-centric forms. The generous aperture and clear counters make extended health reports, consultations, and medical advice effortlessly readable.
- **Labels & Metrics (`Space Grotesk`):** Set with deliberate letter spacing and medium/semibold weights to anchor numerical telemetry, biological metrics, and status pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by an asymmetric 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous horizontal margins and deliberate vertical cadence, mirroring luxury editorial layouts.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, 2rem (32px) gutters, 4rem (64px) minimum outer margins. Maximum layout width is capped at 1440px for optimal line tracking.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, 1.5rem (24px) gutters, 2.5rem (40px) outer margins. Cards dynamically reflow from 3-up to 2-up configurations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, 1rem (16px) gutters, 1.25rem (20px) outer margins. Data widgets shift into horizontal gesture rails with edge snapping.

### Rhythmic Vertical Cadence
Sections use dramatic 4rem to 6rem vertical spacing to evoke an Apple-inspired sense of quiet luxury, ensuring clinical diagnostic elements never feel dense, overcrowded, or anxiety-inducing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows, choosing instead an atmosphere of layered purity using **Tonal Glass Surfaces** and **Bioluminescent Ambient Glows**.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Serene matte off-white background (`#F7F9F8`).
2. **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Panels):** Pure optical white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-diffused, resting shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(16, 32, 39, 0.03)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Focus States):** Card elevation rises with a 1px border shift to `#0878B9` (at 30% alpha) and shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(8, 120, 185, 0.08)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modal & Diagnostic Drawers):** High-density optical white elevated above backdrops using frosted glass blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`) with shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(7, 26, 38, 0.12)`.
5. **Biometric Glow Accent:** Used selectively behind high-performance scorecards or vital summaries. A soft radial bleed: `radial-gradient(circle at top left, rgba(41, 163, 74, 0.12), transparent 70%)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-fluid, organic pill-shaped geometric vocabulary (Level 3), reflecting living cellular structures, ergonomic comfort, and smooth medical hardware.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Elements (Badges, Tags, Buttons, Selectors):** Full pill curvature (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Standard Cards & Input Surfaces:** `rounded-lg` (2rem / 32px), creating friendly, approachable silhouettes for patient interfaces.
- **Hero Containers & Nested Modules:** `rounded-xl` (3rem / 48px), creating distinct visual sanctums for primary clinical features.
- **Nested Inner Components:** Strictly follow proportional concentric curvature (e.g., an outer 32px card houses inner actions with 16px to 20px corners) to ensure optical alignment.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action):** Full pill (`9999px`), background `#29A34A`, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `label-lg`. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: background shifts to `#238B3F` with an ambient green halo (`0 6px 20px rgba(41, 163, 74, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary (Clinical Anchor):** Full pill, background `#071A26`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#0D2B3E`.
- **Tertiary (Outline):** Full pill, 1.5px border `#E2E8F0`, background transparent, text `#071A26`. Hover: background `#FFFFFF`, border `#0878B9`.

### Delicate Pill Badges & Chips
- **Status Badges:** Height 28px, full pill shape, padding `4px 14px`. Typography `label-caps`.
  - *Active / Optimal:* Background `rgba(41, 163, 74, 0.1)`, text `#29A34A`, border `1px solid rgba(41, 163, 74, 0.2)`.
  - *Clinical / Diagnostic:* Background `rgba(8, 120, 185, 0.1)`, text `#0878B9`, border `1px solid rgba(8, 120, 185, 0.2)`.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Full pill, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#102027`. When active: `#071A26` background, `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards
- Standard containers set to `#FFFFFF` with `rounded-lg` (32px), 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, and padding of `32px` (desktop) or `20px` (mobile). Top border incorporates an optional subtle 2px accent gradient line (`#0878B9` to `#29A34A`) for highlighted care pathways.

### Form Inputs
- **Field Wrappers:** Height 52px, pill-curved (`9999px`) or softened pill (`16px`), background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#102027`, typography `body-md`. Padding `0 20px`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#0878B9` with a subtle blue ring (`0 0 0 4px rgba(8, 120, 185, 0.12)`).

### Lists & Telemetry Items
- Borderless list rows with subtle `#E9EEF0` hair-dividers. Active items employ a gentle left pill-indicator in `#29A34A` and hover transitions with `#F7F9F8` fill.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 20px × 20px rounded squares (6px radius). When active: `#29A34A` fill with crisp white vector tick.
- **Radios:** 20px × 20px circle with a solid concentric inner circle in `#0878B9` on selection.
